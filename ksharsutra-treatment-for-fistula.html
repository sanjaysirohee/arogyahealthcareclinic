What is Ksharsutra Treatment for Fistula?

Ksharsutra is a time-tested, specialized, and minimally invasive Ayurvedic para-surgical procedure recognized worldwide as the gold standard for treating complex anal fistulas, pilonidal sinus, and stubborn chronic conditions. At Arogya Healthcare Clinic, our expert practitioners utilize specially prepared medicated threads coated with herbal latex and alkaline extracts to cut, drain, and heal the fistulous tract simultaneously.

This procedure eliminates the need for major open surgery, significantly lowers the risk of incontinence, and ensures complete removal of the infection root with near-zero recurrence rates.
