Advanced Ayurvedic Diagnosis & Care for Rectal Polyps

A rectal polyp is an abnormal growth of tissue starting on the lining of the rectum or colon. While many polyps are benign, early detection and expert medical care are vital to prevent complications, bleeding, and discomfort. At Arogya Healthcare Clinic, we provide careful examination and specialized Ayurvedic management to support long-term colon health.

Our expert practitioners use a holistic approach, blending herbal formulations, dietary corrections, and safe clinical practices to manage underlying inflammation and restore your body's natural balance.

Understanding Growth

Flat vs. Polyp with Stalk

Flat (Sessile) Polyps

Flat polyps grow directly against the mucosal lining of the rectum without any distinct stem or stalk. They require careful screening and targeted management to reduce irritation and inflammation.

Polyps with Stalk (Pedunculated)

Pedunculated polyps grow on a thin stalk protruding from the lining into the inside of the rectum. Our specialists provide precise diagnosis and comprehensive treatment options tailored to each case.
