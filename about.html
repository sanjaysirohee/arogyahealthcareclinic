Meet Doctor

Dr. Gurdeep Singh

Director of the Arogya Healthcare Clinic, is an Ano-Rectal Surgeon in "Proctology" who performs Ayurvedic surgical procedures for Piles, Fistula, Fissure in Ano, Pilonidal Sinus, Rectal Polyp, Rectal Prolapse, etc., through Ayurveda Ksharsutra Surgery. An expert in Ayurvedic panchakarma, agnikarma, and ksharkarma, Dr. Singh has an experience of 32 years in this field.

After completing his degree from Delhi University, Dr. Singh has worked in the prestigious hospitals of Delhi like:

  • Ayurvedic & Unani Tibbia College and Hospital Karol Bagh, New Delhi.
  • Deendayal Upaddhyay Hospital, New Delhi.
  • Hindu Rao Hospital, Delhi.

Dr. Singh has operated on more than 50,000 patients of ano-rectal diseases during his practice of 32 years.

There is no need for hospital stay after the Ayurvedic Para-surgical procedure. The entire procedure is painless without any significant bleeding, and the patient can indulge in his daily routine from the next day of the procedure. Charges for the procedure are quite low as compared to a conventional anorectal surgery. The disease is cured completely after the specialised Ayurvedic surgery by Dr. Singh and does not relapse.
